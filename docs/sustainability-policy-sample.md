# Campus Water & Energy Conservation Guidelines (Sample)

## Water usage

Hostel residents are encouraged to limit shower time to under 5 minutes and to
report leaking taps to facilities within 24 hours. Washing machines should be
run only on full loads. Water coolers should be checked weekly for silent leaks.

## Electricity usage

Lights and fans should be switched off when a room is unoccupied for more than
15 minutes. Chargers should be unplugged when not in use, since idle chargers
still draw standby power. Air conditioning, where available, is recommended to
be set no lower than 24°C to balance comfort and consumption.

## Waste segregation

Waste must be segregated into wet (biodegradable), dry (recyclable), and
hazardous (batteries, e-waste) bins. Dry waste should be rinsed before disposal
to avoid contaminating recyclables. E-waste should be handed to the facilities
desk, not placed in general bins.

## SDG alignment

These guidelines directly support SDG 12 (Responsible Consumption and
Production), SDG 6 (Clean Water and Sanitation), and SDG 7 (Affordable and
Clean Energy) by reducing avoidable resource waste at the source.
